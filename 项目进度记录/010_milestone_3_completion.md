# Milestone 3: Active Challenge Release Completion

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: active five-category release complete; Milestone 4 is next

Milestone 3's active five-category challenge release is complete. Challenge
records remain private and evaluation-only; no challenge text or source paths
are published in this repository.

## Release totals

| Category | Total | Development | Frozen | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Single-location replacement | 300 | 300 | 0 | Development-only; future frozen material follows the recorded SOP |
| Multiple-location mixing | 120 | 60 | 60 | 20 development / 20 frozen per target language |
| Longer shared expressions | 20 | 10 | 10 | Equal development/frozen split |
| Target-distinctive short sentences | 60 | 30 | 30 | 10 development / 10 frozen per target language |
| Shared short sentences | 20 | 10 | 10 | Equal development/frozen split |
| **Total** | **520** | **410** | **110** | |

The active release uses five categories. Alternative-spelling and user-facing
segmentation extensions are deferred. The multiple-location quantity was
reduced to 40 accepted families per target language as an explicit quality
decision rather than padding the release with weaker cases.

Reproducible development/frozen assignment artifacts are retained in the
private repository. Single-location replacement has no frozen portion in this
release; future frozen generation will use the sealed-reference SOP after the
evaluation procedure is frozen.

## Boundary

Milestone 3 did not authorize training, a baseline, a Transformer, tokenizer
work, retrieval, UI, or Phase 2 work. The next authorized milestone is
Milestone 4: the rule-based / character n-gram baseline. It starts only after
discussion, design, and explicit approval.
